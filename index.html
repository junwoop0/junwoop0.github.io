I received my B.S. in Computer Science and Engineering from Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU), where I began my research with Prof. Sujee Lee at the AI-Healthcare Lab.

Outside research, I build apps for campus communities and previously led Google Developer Groups on Campus at SKKU.
