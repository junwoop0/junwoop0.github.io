I am an incoming M.S. student in Computer Science at the University of California, Irvine. I received my B.S. in Computer Science and Engineering from Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU), where I work as a research assistant at the AI-Healthcare Lab, advised by Prof. Sujee Lee.

My research interests center on:

  • AI for healthcare: building robust AI systems for healthcare and biomedical applications.
  • Multimodal LLMs: adapting language models to diverse data modalities.

Outside research, I like building things people actually use, from a campus announcement app to an AI platform for my university’s mascot. I also served as president of Google Developer Groups on Campus at SKKU.

Project SKKU Character

Oct. 2025 – Feb. 2026

AI platform for SKKU’s mascot characters

An AI platform where students create and share artwork of SKKU’s mascot from text prompts. I fine-tuned a FLUX image-generation model on mascot datasets and built the FastAPI backend serving generation requests, funded by the university entrepreneurship program.

NotiSKKU

Aug. 2024 – Nov. 2025

Student announcement app

A mobile app that gathers scattered campus announcements into a single view, motivated by a survey of 60+ students. I led a four-member team, designed the UI/UX in Figma, and built the app in Flutter on top of a Playwright crawler and Firebase. Launched on Google Play.

VisualVroom

Apr. 2025 – Aug. 2025

Alert system for hearing-impaired drivers

A real-time auditory alert system for deaf drivers. We used a Vision Transformer on audio spectrograms to classify both the type (siren, horn) and direction of sounds at 83.9% accuracy, delivering haptic alerts through a smartwatch. In-car communication is supported by Google Speech-to-Text and a Gemini-powered speech-to-sign feature.
