## About Me

Hello! I am Junwoo Park. I recently graduated in Computer Science and Engineering from Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU). I currently work as a Research Assistant at the <a href="https://sites.google.com/view/ai-healthcare-lab" target="_blank">AI-Healthcare Lab</a>, advised by Professor Sujee Lee.

## Research Interests

I work at the intersection of computing and real-world problems, with a focus on:
